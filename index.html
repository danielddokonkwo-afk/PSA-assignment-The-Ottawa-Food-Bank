Good food is health care.

This website explains what the Ottawa Food Bank does. It also talks about who it helps and how people in Ottawa can find food support.

PSA Assignment by Daniel O.

What the Ottawa Food Bank does

The Ottawa Food Bank helps people get food when they do not have enough money.

Purpose

The purpose of the Ottawa Food Bank is to supply people who need food in Ottawa. People can get food through local programs. They can also volunteer or donate food.

Food and essentials

It provides fresh food and canned food. It also helps with diapers, baby food, toiletries, cleaning supplies, etc.

Community action

The Food Bank grows some of its food. It accepts donations from local farmers too. People can run food drives and volunteer to help out.

People working together

Volunteers and donations are a big part of how food gets sorted and shared with people who need it.

Who is served

The Food Bank serves people in Ottawa who cannot afford good food. This includes people going through a rough time financially and working-poor individuals.

OttawaIt serves people in Ottawa. Food programs often serve the area close to where a person lives.
All agesIndividuals and families use the Food Bank. About 37% of the people helped are children. Not getting enough food can affect a child's learning and future.
All gendersPeople of all genders and ages deserve access to healthy food.

Why this matters

When people do not have enough food, it can make everyday life harder. It may be harder to focus at school or work. It can also cause stress. Food support gives people some help when they need it most.

Food insecurity is a health issue

Food insecurity is when someone does not have enough money to buy the food they need. They may worry about food. They might eat less or skip meals.

This can affect a person's body and stress level. It can also make it harder to learn. Food banks help in emergencies. People still need enough income and affordable housing.

Dimensions of health supported

Physical health: balanced food gives people energy. It supports growth and immune function. It can also lower the risk of some chronic health problems. Intellectual health: enough calories and nutrients can improve focus and memory. This is especially important for children in school.

Determinants of health

The Food Bank also helps with two determinants of health:

  • Income and social status: hunger can be a symptom of poverty. The Food Bank helps when people are having financial difficulties.
  • Childhood experiences: proper nutrition can help children grow and develop. It may also prevent delays and long-term health problems.

History and funding

1984

This service was founded in 1984 in the basement of the former police station on Waller Street. It was meant to be temporary. Because poverty and food insecurity continued, it grew into a food-relief organization.

Today

Today it supports food programs across Ottawa. It is a registered charity. It mostly relies on support from the community.

Where the funding comes from

Most funding comes from community support. This includes individual donations, sponsorships, charitable foundations, food donations, fundraising events, etc. The Food Bank also receives some government funding through municipal or provincial grants.

Partners

It works with local food banks and food cupboards. These include neighbourhood groups, community associations, faith-based organizations, shelters, and schools. It also works with Ottawa Public Health, the City of Ottawa, Feed Ontario, and Food Banks Canada.

NEED FOOD SUPPORT?

Find a food program near you.

The Ottawa Food Bank is based in Ottawa and serves people in Ottawa. Its address is 2001 Bantree Street. Use the official finder to find a food program near you. If you need help call 613-745-7001. You can also call 211 for food-support options.

Other food banks serve other cities. For example, Fort York Food Bank and Daily Bread Food Bank serve Toronto.
