What the Ottawa Food Bank does

The Ottawa Food Bank helps people get food when money is tight.

Purpose

The Ottawa Food Bank helps people in Ottawa who need food. People can get food, volunteer, or donate. The Food Bank grows food and accepts donations from local farmers.

Food and essentials

It helps provide fresh food, canned food, and useful items like diapers, baby food, toiletries, and cleaning supplies.

Community action

People can get food through a local program. They can also volunteer, donate food or money, or run a food drive. The Food Bank grows some food and accepts donations from farmers and neighbours.

Who is served

The Food Bank helps people in Ottawa who cannot afford enough good food or who are having a hard time with money.

OttawaIt serves people in Ottawa. Community food programs usually serve the area around a person's home.
All agesIndividuals and families use the Food Bank. About 37% of people helped are children. Not having enough food can affect a child's learning and future.
All gendersPeople of all genders, ages, cultures, and abilities deserve access to healthy food.

Food insecurity is a health issue

Food insecurity is when someone does not have enough money to buy the food they need. This can mean worrying about food, eating less, skipping meals, or sometimes going without food.

It can affect a person's body, stress level, and ability to learn. Food banks help in an emergency, but people also need enough income and affordable housing.

Dimensions of health supported

Physical health: healthy food supports energy, growth, and staying well. Intellectual health: good food helps children learn and develop. Getting food support can also lower stress.

Determinants of health

The Food Bank helps with two important things that affect health:

  • Income and social status: people need enough money for food and other basics.
  • Childhood experiences: children need healthy food to grow and learn.

History and funding

1984

The Ottawa Food Bank started in 1984 in the basement of the old police station on Waller Street. Small food banks and volunteers worked together to start it. It was meant to be temporary, but it grew into an important food-relief organization.

Today

Today, it supports food programs across Ottawa. It is a registered charity and mostly relies on community support.

Where the funding comes from

The funding usually comes from community support, private donations, food donations, fundraising events, grants, and business sponsors.

Partners

It works with local food programs, food banks, shelters, schools, Ottawa Public Health, the City of Ottawa, Feed Ontario, and Food Banks Canada.
